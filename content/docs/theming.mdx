title: Theming
description: One stylesheet holds every token, material and curve.
---

Adding the theme merges everything into your `app/globals.css`: the colour tokens into `:root` and `.dark`, the rest into `@theme inline`, plus the material utilities and base styles. Change a token there and the whole kit follows.

## Colour

The accent comes from three numbers: `--hue` and `--chroma` set the verde, `--tint` warms the stone neutrals. Swap the hue to move the whole kit to another marble.

```css
:root {
  --hue: 165;
  --chroma: 0.07;
  --tint: 80;
}
```

## Materials

| Utility | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `gloss` | The diagonal reflection plus a deep base; `--gloss-strength` scales it |
| `shadow-key` | A keycap with a solid lip in a darker shade of its face (`--lip`) |
| `shadow-slab` | A raised surface: cards, popups, tables |
| `shadow-well` | A recessed surface: inputs, tracks, trays |
| `rim` | The lit top edge of a slab, fading down its sides |
| `marble` / `marble-fine` | Page-scale veining, and the finer streaks used on bands |

## Motion

Three curves, used everywhere: `ease-out` for things entering and leaving, `ease-in-out` for things moving on screen, `ease-drawer` for sheets and drawers. Popups take 150ms, modals 200ms, and nothing driven from the keyboard animates.
